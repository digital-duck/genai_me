# AI Trends

## [Trends - AI](https://www.bondcap.com/reports/tai)
Mary Meeker report on AI - May 2025 by Bond Capital

Meeker, co-founder of the venture capital firm Bond who formerly analyzed technology portfolios for Merrill Lynch, Salomon Brothers, and Morgan Stanley, published “Trends — Artificial Intelligence (May ‘25).” The report, which spans 340 graph-packed pages, revives and updates a series that chronicled the rise of the internet nearly every year from 1995 through 2019.
